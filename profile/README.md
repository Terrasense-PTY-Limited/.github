# TerraSense

### Field comms that keep working where the signal ends

**TerraSense (PTY) Ltd** builds **TerraField**, the mobile app for rangers, anti-poaching units and conservation field teams. Messaging, a live team map, SOS, reports and tasks keep working over a long-range **LoRa radio mesh** when there is no cell signal, and sync to the cloud when a connection returns.

## What we build

**TerraField app (iOS and Android).** An offline-first field operations app for game reserves and protected areas.
- **Team messaging** in direct, group and team channels, over the radio mesh and the internet
- **Live team map** with positions and trails, even with no cell signal
- **SOS** that reaches the whole team over the radio mesh or the internet
- **Reports and observations** with photos, voice notes and GPS, saved offline and synced later
- **Tasks and patrols** assigned from the field or from the office
- **HeyTerra**, an assistant you can ask by voice or text who is closest, who was speeding or where a sighting was
- **Speaks your team's language:** English, Afrikaans, Zulu, Swahili, French and Portuguese, with on-device voice transcription

**TerraSense radios.** Long-range LoRa field radios that pair with the app over Bluetooth and form a mesh across the reserve. Built on [Meshtastic](https://meshtastic.org) firmware.

**TerraSense Portal.** A web control room for reserve managers: live map, reports, tasks, attendance, vehicle activity, speed alerts and reserve rules.

## How it works

```
Phone ──Bluetooth──▶ TerraSense radio ══ LoRa mesh (kilometres, no SIM) ══▶ other radios and phones
                                              │
                                    Gateway, when one is online
                                              ▼
                                     Cloud and TerraSense Portal
```

When a ranger has signal, everything goes over the internet. When the signal drops, the same message, position or SOS goes out over the radio mesh. Nothing is lost, and nothing needs to be sent twice.
